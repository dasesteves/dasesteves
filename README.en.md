**From biology to software, with a fascination for systems.**

I trained as a biologist and hold master's degrees in **Bioinformatics** and in **Molecular Biology, Biotechnology and Bioentrepreneurship in Plants** from the University of Minho. I now develop **software for healthcare** at Santa Casa da Misericórdia de Vila Verde: interfaces with React and TypeScript, services and APIs with Node.js, legacy systems integration and work with SQL databases.

My research in microbiology, molecular biology and biotechnology <code>(plant biology, microalgae and aquaculture)</code> took me to the **Portuguese Institute for Sea and Atmosphere** (IPMA) and the **Centre of Marine Sciences** (CCMAR) in the Algarve.

Here I bring together code and projects. My professional background is on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/dteves/); my research record and publications are on [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5741-5686) and [CiênciaVitae](https://www.cienciavitae.pt/en/FA15-C164-E57B).
